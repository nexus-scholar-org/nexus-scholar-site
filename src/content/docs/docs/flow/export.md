---
title: Export and methods
description: Download the decisions as RIS or CSV and copy a draft methods paragraph written from the workspace files.
---

After publishing, **Export and methods** appears on the screening home and on
the Publish screen.

## Download the decisions

- **RIS**: the included and unclear records, to import into Zotero, EndNote or
  Mendeley for full-text retrieval.
- **CSV**: every record, its decision and its reason code.

## Copy the methods paragraph

**Copy paragraph** gives a methods paragraph with your PRISMA counts, the
databases and dates you searched, and how AI drafts were used. It names the
reporting guideline (PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR or none, set from your review type
or chosen in the protocol) and the duplicate-matching rules in force, and it
says whether those values come from your signed protocol or are the defaults.

It is a draft written from your workspace files: check every number before you
use it.

## The PRISMA counts

The Import step gives the identification counts: records found in each
database, duplicates removed, and records to screen. The published screening
gives the rest: records screened, excluded with the reason breakdown, included,
and unclear (sent to full text). The PRISMA screening report in the workspace
holds the same numbers.
