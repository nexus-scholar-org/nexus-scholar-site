title: Export and methods
description: Download the decisions as RIS or CSV and copy a draft methods paragraph written from the workspace files.
---

After publishing, **Export and methods** appears on the screening home and on
the Publish screen.

## Download the decisions

- **RIS**: the included and unclear records, to import into Zotero, EndNote or
  Mendeley for full-text retrieval.
- **CSV**: every record, its decision and its reason code.

## Copy the methods paragraph

**Copy paragraph** gives a methods paragraph with your PRISMA counts, the
databases and dates you searched, and how AI drafts were used. It names the
reporting guideline (PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR or none, set from your review type
or chosen in the protocol) and the duplicate-matching rules in force, and it
says whether those values come from your signed protocol or are the defaults.

Since 1.1.0b7 the paragraph also reports criteria and method amendments, the
pilot, a combined corpus and the declaration of decisions stored from drafts.

It is a draft written from your workspace files: check every number before you
use it.

## The PRISMA counts

The Import step gives the identification counts: records found in each
database, duplicates removed, and records to screen. The published screening
gives the rest: records screened, excluded with the reason breakdown, included,
and unclear (sent to full text). The PRISMA screening report in the workspace
holds the same numbers.

## After publishing: Charting

<p class="ns-beta"><strong>New in 1.1.0b7,</strong> and used only on test data apart from a first look on one real review.</p>

The Charting step lists every kept study (Include and Unclear, from the search
and from chase rounds). For each you record the full-text status, attach a PDF
or paste a link, and record a full-text decision (Include, or Exclude with one
reason). You fill your protocol's charting columns in the table or one study at
a time; "Not reported" and "Not applicable" are values, and each cell has a
note and "where from". Changes are append-only events, and **Z** undoes your
last change.

**Look up open-access copies** (`L`) shows first what it will ask (Unpaywall
with your contact e-mail; OpenAlex only with `SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY`; arXiv for
a study with an arXiv id and no DOI), then fetches only PDFs those services
list as open access. Each download names your contact e-mail to the website
that holds the PDF. Each fetched PDF waits as
"Fetched, to confirm" until you open and confirm it. Publishers refuse
automated downloads, so expect "Not retrieved" or a link to open yourself for
many studies. Reading a PDF's text is parked: the form is filled by you.

The charting export gives a spreadsheet (`.xlsx`), a full-text CSV and a
charting CSV, the PRISMA full-text counts per round and in total, and a methods
paragraph whose numbers are computed from the events.
