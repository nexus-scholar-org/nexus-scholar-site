Released on 2026-11-23, on top of [1.1.0b6](../1-1-0b6/). Superseded by
[1.1.0b8](../1-1-0b8/). Install or upgrade:

```bash
uv tool install --force "nexus-scholar>=1.1.0b7"
nexus-scholar --version   # nexus-scholar 1.1.0b7
```

Do not install with `--prerelease allow` (see [1.1.0b4](../1-1-0b4/)).
**Keep a copy of each workspace folder before you open it with this build**
(see "Known limits"). This beta also **removes** two packages, their commands
and eight MCP tools: read "Removed, and what breaks" before you upgrade if you
use the command line or an AI client over MCP.

Each item's evidence is in the
[milestone records](https://github.com/nexus-scholar-org/nexus-scholar-harness/tree/main/docs/roadmap/milestones)
of the product repository. A walk of this build on test data is in
[`R-1.1.0b7.md`](https://github.com/nexus-scholar-org/nexus-scholar-harness/blob/v1.1.0b7/docs/roadmap/milestones/R-1.1.0b7.md).

## What is new for you

### Inception (before the protocol is signed)

- **Explore and Direction have screens.** Explore searches a first sample of
  papers per database; Direction offers directions drawn from phrases in at
  least two papers of that sample. Synonyms from the sample are accepted one by
  one; papers you anchor become recall seeds. Explore and Direction send your
  topic to the databases, so they need `SCHOLAR_MAILTO` (and, for OpenAlex,
  `SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY`). See [Inception](../../flow/inception/).
- **Search quality and database choice.** A keyword query is derived from your
  topic without AI, shown per database and editable. A database that returned
  nothing says "0 results". You choose and order the databases in Explore and
  in Discovery; the order sets the sample priority only, never which record
  represents a duplicate group.
- **Checks and suggestions on every step.** A Checks panel with about 50 fixed
  checks (no AI) on Inception and Discovery; suggestions you Apply, Edit or
  Reject, kept in `audit/proposals.jsonl`; a "what's next" bar on every screen;
  the planned number of records on Shape; your own charting columns; **Start
  from an existing review** (a workspace's protocol or a protocol file).
- **The query workbench on step 5.** While you edit concepts and synonyms,
  **Count** shows, per database, the exact string sent, its total and the count
  with each concept dropped. A seed check says whether your known papers can
  test the query. A database export staged as a comparison set shows how many
  of its records your query misses. Every button says how many requests it
  sends; nothing is counted while you type.
- **How you will screen is asked at step 5:** when to see the agent's draft,
  and what a draft does when the abstract does not report a criterion. No
  choice is preselected; Review and Create refuse until you choose. Five
  wording checks flag criteria that mirror or contradict each other, hold
  several tests or need the full text.
- **Lighter screens:** tips behind an icon, one main action per step, the
  recommended choice marked and never preselected.
- **An assistant and a critic over MCP.** Five `nexus_assistant_*` tools let an
  AI client read drafts and workspaces, propose changes and report findings,
  which appear as suggestions and check rows. No tool searches, accepts,
  decides or publishes. **Not yet tried with a real AI client.**

### Search and corpus (Discovery)

- **One corpus from a harvest and imported exports.** A browser harvest and
  staged RIS or `.nbib` exports are deduplicated together into one snapshot, in
  any order. Imported records are not checked against Crossref or OpenAlex and
  their missing abstracts are not filled in; the Hand-off and the methods text
  say so.
- **Change the search from Plan.** The workbench opens on Plan; "Record this
  amendment" needs a one-sentence reason and lists what the amendment makes
  stale.
- **A harvest says which stage it is in, and can be finished.** Harvest and
  Hand-off say when the preparation after the search is unfinished, failed or
  stopped, also after a restart; **Finish preparing this corpus** completes it
  from the saved records without searching again. The accept refuses an
  unfinished preparation.
- **A size estimate before a harvest:** a count per database and a suggested
  cap on Plan and Harvest.
- **Citation chasing.** After the screening is published, **Chase** (Discovery
  step 8, or `nexus-scholar chase run|status|accept`) fetches the references
  and the citing papers of your included studies (OpenAlex first, Semantic
  Scholar second), keeps only what is new to the corpus, and sends it to a
  second screening round. The PRISMA report gains a "citation searching" arm.
  One level per run.
- **Provider fixes.** PubMed searches `[tiab]`; OpenAlex needs its key and pages
  at most 100; a rejected Semantic Scholar key fails its leg without a keyless
  retry; shared request budgets per database; the arXiv leg sends the year
  range.
- **Protocol-change signals.** Screening Home says when the protocol changed
  after screening started instead of showing an empty queue; the Hand-off warns
  before the accept.

### Screening

- **Triage highlights.** The protocol's concepts and query terms are marked in
  the title and abstract, and, once the draft is shown, the spans the agent's
  draft quoted, tied to their criterion. Two switches turn each kind off. A
  mark is never colour alone.
- **The criteria pilot.** On Prepare, **Pilot your criteria first** draws 20 to
  100 records at random (recorded seed); you screen them in Triage; pilot
  decisions are kept apart and do not count; a result screen shows which
  criteria decided your exclusions. Before the first batch is approved you can
  revise a criterion as a recorded amendment beside the signed protocol; the
  methods paragraph lists each amendment.
- **Approve every decided batch from one paste.** On Publish, **Show the codes
  for every decided batch** prints one line per batch in the terminal (and in a
  small desktop window with `nexus-scholar ui`); paste them all at once. Each
  approval still binds its own batch.
- **Decisions stored from the agent's drafts are declared.** The new screen
  **Decisions stored from the agent's draft** lets you declare decisions you
  stored from drafts without reading each record, and draw a sample of the
  adopted exclusions to read. Declared decisions are left out of Agreement's
  figures; Publish shows the check "Decisions stored from drafts are declared";
  the methods paragraph states how many records you read, how many decisions
  came from drafts and what a sample found. The decisions themselves are not
  changed.
- **A decisive answer can decide a draft.** A third not-reported rule, "a
  decisive answer first" (`decisive_first`). Blinding and the not-reported rule
  can be changed from Prepare after the protocol is signed, as a recorded
  amendment.
- **Faster screening pages.** Home, Publish and the PRISMA preview read the
  decision log once per request. On a generated 1,062-record workspace they
  answer in 0.2 to 0.4 s instead of 2.1 to 3.3 s (not measured on a real
  review).

### Full text and charting (new)

- **A Charting step after publication.** The table lists every kept study
  (Include and Unclear, from the search and from chase rounds). For each:
  full-text status (To get, Requested, Retrieved, Not retrieved with one
  reason), **Attach a PDF** (stored as `pdfs/<study_id>.pdf`) or **Paste a
  link**, and a full-text decision (Include, or Exclude with one reason).
  Changes are append-only events; **Z** undoes your last change.
- **The charting form and the one-study view.** Your protocol's columns, filled
  by keyboard in the table or one study at a time (`J`/`K` or
  `Ctrl+↓`/`Ctrl+↑`); "Not reported" and "Not applicable" are values; each cell
  has a note and "where from". The first studies pilot the form; a later change
  to it is a recorded amendment.
- **Look up open-access copies** (`L`). Shows first what it will ask (Unpaywall
  with your contact e-mail; OpenAlex only with `SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY`), then
  fetches only PDFs those services list as open access. Each fetched PDF waits
  as "Fetched, to confirm" until you open and confirm it. It refuses before any
  request without `SCHOLAR_MAILTO`.
- **`scholar-pdf download` and `ingest` are production.** They read every
  open-access location both services list, name the copy chosen, write a
  manifest row per DOI and say why a DOI has no file. They need
  `SCHOLAR_MAILTO` and refuse a proxy.

### Export and publication

- **Charting export and methods:** a spreadsheet (`.xlsx`, every text cell
  written as text), a full-text CSV and a charting CSV, the PRISMA full-text
  counts per round and in total, and a methods paragraph whose numbers are
  computed from the events.
- The screening methods paragraph now reports criteria and method amendments,
  the pilot, a combined corpus and the declaration of decisions stored from
  drafts.

## What changed in behaviour

- **Default databases:** OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref and arXiv.
  **PubMed is off by default** (still selectable); a protocol that names its
  databases runs exactly those. Crossref is not ticked for a query that joins
  two or more concepts, and Plan warns while it is.
- **OpenAlex refuses without a key:** a browser harvest that selects OpenAlex
  without `SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY` is refused with `OPENALEX_KEY_NOT_SET`.
- **A provider key in an environment variable is used before one in the system
  keychain**; `nexus-scholar keys status` says which one is in use.
- **A used-up provider quota no longer hangs the checks** after a harvest: the
  step ends unfinished with the database and its reset time, and Finish
  preparing checks only the rest.
- **Agent runs name their provider, model and cost ceiling.** A run or a
  calibration is refused without `provider`, `model` and `cost_ceiling_usd` (a
  number, or `null` for no ceiling). There is no default model any more. The
  Agent proposals screen sends all three; a blank ceiling field sends `null`.
- **"AI-agreed exclusion" is refused.** Saving the mode `ai_agreed_exclusion`
  answers a refusal; every record is seen by the reviewer. The Prepare screen
  still offers the option and then shows the refusal.
- **The not-reported rule has no default for a new review.** `nexus-scholar
  init` and `inception` take `--not-reported unclear|decisive_first|exclude`,
  and a scripted interview without it is refused.
- **The Import step has no accept button;** you accept the corpus on the
  Hand-off.
- **Publish's first check is renamed** "Every decision is recorded as the
  reviewer's".
- **`openpyxl` is a base dependency.**
- **Request limits:** request bodies, inception drafts and decision notes
  (2,000 characters) have size limits; a decision whose time is more than 5
  minutes after the engine received it is refused.

## Removed, and what breaks

Removed on 2026-11-23:

- **Packages:** `scholar_rag` (indexing, retrieval, synthesis, the evidence
  matrix) and `scholar_verify` (retraction, open science, conflicts of
  interest, risk of bias). The wheel now carries five kits: protocol, search,
  PDF, graph, agent.
- **Commands:** `scholar-rag` and `scholar-verify` with all their subcommands;
  `nexus-scholar extract index`; the `--index` option of `nexus-scholar
  extract publish`. `extract status` and `extract publish` remain.
- **Extra:** `rag`. `all` is now `pdf` plus `extract`. An install line naming
  `[rag]` fails.
- **MCP tools (8):** `nexus_rag_index`, `nexus_rag_query`,
  `nexus_rag_synthesize`, `nexus_matrix_extract`, `nexus_verify_claims`,
  `nexus_verify_phase4`, `nexus_critique_methodology` and `nexus_screen_llm`.
  With the five new `nexus_assistant_*` tools the server registers **24** tools
  (27 in 1.1.0b6). An AI client configured for a removed tool gets "unknown
  tool".
- **HTTP route:** `POST /api/v1/screening/batch/{n}/decisions`. Decisions are
  recorded only through `POST /screening/decisions/events`.
- **Pipelines:** `nexus-scholar run` and `nexus_pipeline_run` after a collected
  screening run extraction and the citation graph only; a stored pipeline that
  names `scholar-rag` or `scholar-verify` is refused (`COMMAND_NOT_ALLOWED`).
  `nexus-scholar status` no longer prints the vector-chunk, matrix and
  synthesis rows.
- **Old workspaces** keep their `rag/` and `phase4/` folders; nothing reads
  them.

To get the removed packages back, follow
[`H-remove-rag-verify.md`](https://github.com/nexus-scholar-org/nexus-scholar-harness/blob/v1.1.0b7/docs/roadmap/milestones/H-remove-rag-verify.md)
in a source checkout, or install 1.1.0b6 in a separate environment.

## What was fixed

- A used-up provider quota made the checks after a harvest wait for hours and
  the screen said "Preparing".
- A React error (#418) on every full page load of the packaged app.
- Verified records lost the database that found them; a title-matched record
  without a DOI could be quarantined.
- An old keychain key was used before a good environment key, so model calls
  failed with "invalid x-api-key".
- The open-access resolver read only the "best" location and missed listed
  PDFs.
- In the one-study charting view the focus was lost after `Ctrl+↓`; the
  charting table's rows were five lines tall and the focused row's frame was
  cut.
- Inception: a typed inclusion also wrote a fallback proof criterion;
  colour-only and generic phrases were offered as directions; a cancelled agent
  run did not say it was stopping.
- Discovery: the Crossref, arXiv and cap advice of the acceptance run, and the
  `scholar-search search` exit codes.
- A chase whose accept failed left the workspace locked; a failure after the
  search left the Hand-off empty; a changed protocol showed an empty queue.
- UI wording and time-zone fixes from a browser check of the build; a repeated
  identical publish wrote two journal rows.

## Known limits

The limits of [1.1.0b6](../1-1-0b6/) and the [known limits page](../../known-limits/)
still apply, except where this note says otherwise. Also:

- **Most of what is new was used only on test data.** One real review was taken
  through this code from 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-23: inception, the harvest and
  import, deduplication, agent drafts, screening and publication, then a first
  look at Charting and one live PDF download. The query workbench, the criteria
  pilot, citation chasing, the open-access look-up from Charting, the charting
  form and export, the declaration of decisions stored from drafts and the MCP
  assistant have not been used on a real review. No live chase was run.
- **No automated test opens the app in a browser.** The browser test suite was
  removed on 2026-11-23. The component and screen-state tests remain; the walk
  in `R-1.1.0b7.md` is the only browser check of this build, by one person, on
  test data.
- **Workspaces have no format version.** A newer build may write files an
  older one does not read, and nothing warns you. Copy a workspace folder
  before you open it with this build, and do not go back to 1.1.0b6 on a
  workspace this build has written. (1.1.0b8 adds a format version and a copy
  before the first open.)
- **Opening another workspace from Workspaces can leave the app stopped.** If
  the page stays on "Opening …", close the terminal of `nexus-scholar ui` and
  start it again with `nexus-scholar ui --workspace <the folder>`. (Fixed in
  1.1.0b8.)
- **Nothing is signed, and there is no installer yet.** Install with `uv` or
  `pip` as above. (1.1.0b8 adds an unsigned Windows installer.)
- **Publishers refuse automated downloads.** The look-up asks only the
  open-access services and downloads only the PDFs they list; PubMed Central
  refused its PDF to the app in the live check of 2026-11-22, and the app does
  not pose as a browser. Expect "Not retrieved" or a link to open yourself for
  many studies.
- **The French and Arabic wording of the new screens** was written during the
  builds and not read by a speaker.
- **Reading a PDF's text is still parked:** the charting form is filled by you.
- **The agent's measured thresholds are not decided.** Drafts stay advisory;
  the methods paragraph states how decisions were made.
- **One reviewer, desktop only.** Phone access is not built.
