Released on 2026-11-08. Superseded by [1.1.0b4](../1-1-0b4/) (an install fix)
and [1.1.0b5](../1-1-0b5/).

**Install note, added after release.** Do not install with `--prerelease
allow`: it also takes development releases of other packages, and the app then
stops at start with `No module named 'httpcore'`. Install the current beta
instead:

```bash
uv tool install --force "nexus-scholar>=1.1.0b5"
```

## What changed for you

- **Fast keyboard triage no longer drops a decision.** A key pressed just as
  the next record appeared could reach the previous record and be lost. If you
  screened with the keyboard in 1.1.0b2, check that your counts match your
  queue; the decision history shows each event.
- **More RIS exports import fully.** Authors written as `A1` (Web of Science
  and EndNote-style exports) are read, and titles and abstracts that wrap onto
  a second line arrive whole. Windows line endings are handled.
- **The method values are now in your protocol.** New workspaces state them in
  the protocol: the duplicate-matching rules (DOI, title similarity, first
  author, year tolerance); the screening rules version; what a criterion "not
  reported" does (send to full text, or exclude); and whether the agent's
  draft is hidden until you decide. The methods paragraph reports the values in
  force and says whether they come from your signed protocol or are the
  defaults.
- **Reporting follows a guideline.** The guideline is PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR
  or none, set from your review type or chosen in the protocol. Publish and
  Export name it, and the methods paragraph says which guideline the screening
  is reported under. The counts and files are unchanged.
- **When the protocol sets blinding,** Prepare shows it read-only.
- **Safer saving.** Settings, the approver name and batch approvals are
  written whole or not at all. On Windows, a file briefly locked by antivirus
  or the indexer is retried.

## Existing workspaces

Nothing in an existing workspace changes. A protocol without a method block
keeps its exact fingerprint, so your decisions and publications stay bound to
it. It runs on the defaults, which are the 1.1.0b2 behaviour, and the methods
paragraph says so.

## For anyone calling the local API

Four routes that no screen called were removed; the health check answers a
status only; the settings route gained read-only fields for the blinding
source, the not-reported policy and the reporting guideline; and two refusal
codes were added, `PROTOCOL_METHOD_INVALID` and `BLINDING_SET_BY_PROTOCOL`.

## Known limits

The limits of 1.1.0b2 still apply. Two are new: the Import screen says
"PRISMA" under every guideline, and Prepare has no link to the protocol yet.
